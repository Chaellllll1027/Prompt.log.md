Pack for your household

Emergency kit guide

Store essentials together somewhere easy to reach, and check supplies regularly.

Water

Keep safe drinking water for each person; hydration matters when services are disrupted.

Food

Choose shelf-stable, ready-to-eat food and a manual can opener if needed.

Light and power

A flashlight, spare batteries, and a charged power bank help during outages.

First aid and medicine

Pack basic first-aid supplies and a personal supply of essential medicines.

Documents and cash

Protect copies of key records in a waterproof pouch; keep some small bills.

Personal essentials

Include hygiene items, a whistle, masks, and supplies for children, older adults, or pets.

Simple reminders

Evacuation and safety

General learning prompts for before, during, and after a hazard.

Before a disaster

  • Know your household meeting place and a contact outside your area.
  • Ask your barangay about local evacuation routes and designated shelters.
  • Charge devices, prepare your kit, and plan for household members who need extra support.

During a disaster

  • Stay calm and follow instructions from local authorities and emergency responders.
  • Move to a safer place when advised; avoid floodwater and damaged structures.
  • Keep your household together and use your contact plan when it is safe to do so.

After a disaster

  • Wait for local officials to say it is safe before returning to affected areas.
  • Avoid fallen wires, unstable buildings, and standing water that may be contaminated.
  • Check on neighbors if safe, and use clean water and trusted public updates.
